Since 1968

Founded on Quality & Pride in Workmanship

Founded in 1968, Flex-Hose Co. roots began as a manufacturer of metal hose assemblies in Syracuse, NY, and developed a foundation based on the simple principles of quality and pride in workmanship. Now, over 50 years later, Flex-Hose still maintains and emphasizes those same basic principles in day-to-day operations, management directives, and overall corporate culture. Whether you are looking for a product to absorb vibration, reduce noise, address thermal growth, deal with seismic shift, or resolve pipe misalignment, Flex-Hose Co. can provide a quality, reliable solution to fit your needs.

Why Flex-Hose

"Here at Flex-Hose Company, we understand that no customer's requirements are exactly the same — and that our customer base is comprised of engineers, designers and construction contractors who are responsible for overseeing costs and quality related to the installation of piping systems to existing and newly constructed buildings.

That's why Flex-Hose Co. pays so much attention to the details of your experience. Our employees will greet you in such a way that says we truly care and appreciate the opportunity to meet your performance expectation. Flex-Hose Co. personnel have the technical training and ability to provide solutions for the most demanding application. Our support also extends through specific job analysis services, which recommends placement of product location in a piping system, perfectly integrating them into the overall theme of your project, saving you both time and money. This is the result of staying true to what is essentially right.

It's not very hard to understand why our customers seek innovation, long-term reliability, ease of installation, and extreme cost savings: It's a no-brainer. Not by coincidence, these very same characteristics have been part of the fabric of our business and products since 1968. Flex-Hose sets the standards for innovation and engineering brilliance with the Tri-Flex Loop®, quite literally the envy of the industry. Our renowned products are also backed by the best warranties in the business — and a commitment to quality that other manufacturers find easier to talk about than to duplicate.

Finally, Flex-Hose Company operates under a CSA-B51, Category D, quality plan. This plan is certified by TSSA (Technical Standards and Safety Authority) of Canada. We can apply CRN's to all products that we manufacture, for all Canadian Provinces. In addition to our quality plan we utilize welders trained, and third party tested, in accordance with Section IX of the ASME BPVC (Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code)."

We Manufacture Products That Are Tested, Listed and/or Approved Under:

  • FM (Factory Mutual, fire protection)
  • IAPMO: NSF/ANSI 61 / 372 (National Sanitary Foundation / American National Standards Institute, for drinking water and no lead)
  • UL (Underwriters Laboratories, UL-213 22 for fire protection)
  • UL (Underwriters Laboratories, UL-536 for compressed and combustible gases)
  • Seismic application approval for New York City building codes
  • Seismic application approval for Los Angeles city codes

  • Operate under CSA B51 Quality Plan Authorized by TSSA (Technical Standards & Safety Authority)
  • Compliant with IBC (International Building Code) sections 1613 and 1613.1
  • Compliant with ASCE/SEI (American Society of Civil Engineers) Standard 7-22

Data Centers

Mission-Critical Infrastructure

Today's Digital World Runs on Data Centers

Massive facilities that never sleep. Flex-Hose supplies components for mission-critical systems including server cooling and backup power infrastructure. A data center houses computing, storage, and networking equipment operating continuously to support cloud platforms, financial systems, and healthcare resources — requiring highly controlled environments with redundant power, cooling systems, and fire protection, and the flexible connectors and vibration-isolation solutions that keep them running.

Applications in Data Centers

  • Building separations meeting IBC/ASCE standards
  • Chilled water systems for server cooling
  • HVAC connections
  • Backup power systems
  • Vibration isolation for pumps and chillers
